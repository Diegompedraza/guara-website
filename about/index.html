ABOUT GUARA PLATFORM

A modular Edge AI platform for understanding human state over time.

GUARA Platform combines computer vision, local processing, observability and temporal reasoning to interpret relevant changes in human state in environments where safety and operational continuity matter.

From isolated signals to evolving state

GUARA is designed around a simple idea: a person’s operational state is not adequately represented by a single frame or isolated event. The platform analyzes signals as they evolve, combining current observations with temporal context to build a more useful interpretation of attention, fatigue, orientation, response and related variables.

Edge-first by design

Processing close to the source can reduce dependence on continuous connectivity and support deployments where latency, privacy, bandwidth or infrastructure constraints matter. GUARA therefore treats Edge AI as a core deployment principle rather than an afterthought.

One foundation, multiple verticals

Mobility is the first operational vertical. The same architectural separation between perception, observability, temporal reasoning and vertical intelligence is intended to support future adaptation to industrial operations, mining, aviation and control-room environments.

Responsible validation

The platform is being developed incrementally. Public descriptions distinguish current capabilities from roadmap items and avoid presenting laboratory or preliminary evidence as broad real-world generalization.
