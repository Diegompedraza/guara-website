WORK WITH GUARA

One intelligence layer. Several ways to adopt it.

GUARA’s capabilities can be evaluated on infrastructure you already operate, explored for integration into your products, or deployed as a complete solution. Each path starts with a technical and commercial conversation.

Software licensing Open to licensing evaluation

You already have the infrastructure. GUARA provides the intelligence. If you already operate cameras, Edge hardware or industrial systems, we can evaluate whether GUARA capabilities could be deployed on your infrastructure under a software licensing model. Compatibility is confirmed through a technical assessment.

Technology integration Open to integration

For telematics providers, fleet platforms, software companies and integrators. GUARA is open to evaluating integration models with partners, from local execution to interfaces that deliver state and event information into existing systems. Tell us about your architecture.

Integration tooling, including SDK/API-style interfaces, is under development as part of GUARA’s modular software evolution. Today, integrations are scoped and evaluated project by project.

OEM / Embedded Partnership conversations open

For manufacturers of cameras, Edge devices, vehicles, machinery and industrial equipment. GUARA is open to Embedded and technology-partnership conversations, from early technical assessment to potential joint development. Factory-level automotive integration involves additional validation, homologation and cybersecurity processes, which are addressed case by case.

Complete GUARA deployment Demonstrated · professional mobility

For organizations without existing infrastructure: local processing, GUARA software, event management and deployment hardware when required, delivered through GUARA Professional, the first complete reference implementation. An alternative path, not GUARA’s global identity.

Pilots and proofs of concept

Operational pilots, technology pilots on client hardware, integration tests with client platforms and OEM / manufacturer proofs of concept. Every pilot begins with an agreed scope, a technical assessment, success criteria and data and privacy rules.

Partners and channels

GUARA can reach operators directly or through technology companies that already provide their infrastructure, products or distribution channels.

Tell us what you have

We’ll evaluate the best way to start: licensing, integration, OEM / Embedded, a pilot or a complete deployment.
