Human-State Intelligence for professional mobility.

Professional mobility is where GUARA is demonstrated today. GUARA Professional, the first complete reference implementation of GUARA Platform, applies driver monitoring for fatigue, drowsiness and attention to drivers, fleets and transport operations.

GUARA Professional: the reference implementation

GUARA Professional demonstrates GUARA technology running locally on real Edge hardware in professional mobility. It combines local processing, real-time operational alerts and event reporting in a complete deployment. For organizations without existing infrastructure, it is also the most direct way to evaluate the complete GUARA stack. Hardware and installation are defined for each deployment.

It proves that the intelligence works under real conditions. The software layer behind it is what GUARA intends to scale across infrastructure and sectors.

Beyond a reactive driver-monitoring event

Traditional driver-monitoring interfaces often focus on detecting a specific event. GUARA adds temporal context: how signals persist, recover, repeat or combine. This supports a more continuous view of driver fatigue, drowsiness and attention rather than relying only on isolated detections.

The problem in context

Road crashes are multicausal, and GUARA does not attribute them to a single factor. Within that context, Argentine and regional public agencies identify fatigue, drowsiness, attention, vigilance and reaction time as relevant dimensions for operational safety.

Professional transport context

Potential deployment contexts include passenger transport, freight, logistics, long-distance operations and fleets. The objective is not to replace existing vehicle safety systems, but to add a human-state layer that complements operational safety processes.
