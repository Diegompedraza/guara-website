01 · WHAT GUARA IS

Software intelligence for human state.

GUARA Platform is software intelligence applied to human state. It interprets how the state of people performing critical tasks, or supervising automated systems, evolves during an operation. It turns that into capabilities that can be evaluated on existing infrastructure or deployed as a complete solution.

A camera captures visual signals. GUARA interprets how signals such as eye openness, blinking, gaze and head movement evolve over time, rather than relying on a single frame.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 01

    Observe

    A camera captures visual signals: eyes, blinking, gaze and head orientation.

  2. 02

    Interpret over time

    AI analyzes how those signals evolve during the operation, not isolated moments.

  3. 03

    Process locally

    Analysis runs on the device. No continuous video needs to leave it.

  4. 04

    Deliver operational information

    Alerts, operational state and events for the operator or the GUARA dashboard. For third-party platforms, integration is evaluated per project.

02 · THE PROBLEM

Human risk does not appear all at once.
It builds.

Fatigue, drowsiness and distraction develop during a shift. Whether a person drives a truck, operates heavy machinery or supervises a control room or a remote operation, the early signals are subtle, and most systems only react once an event has already happened.

GUARA focuses on that layer of the problem: how human state changes over time in people who operate or supervise critical tasks.

08 · AUTOMATION & AUTONOMY
VISION

From operator to supervisor.

As tasks become more automated and systems gain autonomy, people increasingly move from executing the task to monitoring it, detecting exceptions and deciding when to intervene. That role still depends on attention. GUARA's long-term direction is to support this transition by interpreting the state of the people who supervise automated processes, teleoperated equipment and control centers.

A development direction, not a current deployment.

PRIVACY & OPERATOR TRUST

Local processing. Minimal data.

GUARA analyzes visual signals on the device. It does not need to identify the person and does not require sending continuous video to the cloud. Local processing reduces the need to transmit video and supports privacy-oriented, compliance-conscious architectures.

  • Local processing

    Analysis runs on the Edge device. Critical detection keeps working offline.

  • No facial recognition

    Evaluates operational-state signals, not who the operator is.

  • No continuous video upload

    Images are processed locally. No continuous video stream is sent to the cloud.

  • Minimized telemetry

    Only operational events and metrics, and only when the configuration requires it.

Does it identify me?
No. The system does not require facial recognition to evaluate operational state.
Does it need Internet?
No. Detection can run locally on the device.
Is it recording me all the time?
Not necessarily. The architecture can process images locally without storing continuous video.
What can be transmitted?
Operational events and metrics when the configuration requires it.
How GUARA protects privacy→

Designed for operational safety, not worker surveillance.
