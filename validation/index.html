VALIDATION

Evidence before claims.

GUARA follows an incremental validation path that separates development evidence from independent evaluation and preserves traceability between data, metrics and technical decisions.

Controlled testing Demonstrated

Laboratory and dataset-based work identifies failure modes, tests temporal behavior and verifies implementation consistency. It is not treated as a substitute for field evidence.

Edge implementation Demonstrated

Running GUARA locally on real embedded hardware exposes compute, timing, camera, lighting and reliability constraints that desktop-only experiments do not reveal.

Field evaluation Ongoing

Evaluation in real driving conditions is required to assess robustness under vibration, changing light, long shifts and operational variability. Claims should remain proportional to the evidence actually collected.

Protocols Ongoing

Documented test protocols keep data, metrics and decisions traceable so that results can be reviewed and reproduced.

Next milestone: independent review Next milestone

Independent technical and academic review of protocols, evidence and limitations is the next validation milestone. Its purpose is to make validation auditable rather than relying only on internal demonstrations.
